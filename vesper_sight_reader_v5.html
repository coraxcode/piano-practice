02 / THE CHALLENGE

A sharp (♯) raises a note by one semitone; a flat (♭) lowers it by one semitone. A natural (♮) means the unaltered note. Read the written spelling, not just the sound. In this single-note drill, a natural sign can be shown as a courtesy reminder, even without an earlier accidental. In a full score, it often cancels a key-signature or earlier accidental.
